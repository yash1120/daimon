Daimon

A daily correspondence with history’s great philosophers.

Daimon is an ongoing exchange of letters with Seneca, Marcus Aurelius, Nietzsche, Camus, and Simone Weil. Each writes you a short letter in their own voice; you reply when you like; over time it becomes a correspondence of your own.

  • A letter arrives A philosopher writes to you, in their own voice.
  • You reply Your replies shape what comes next.
  • Hold a salon Put one question to several philosophers at once.
  • Grounded in their works Answers draw on the philosophers’ actual writings.

Your correspondence is your own — private to you, and free.
